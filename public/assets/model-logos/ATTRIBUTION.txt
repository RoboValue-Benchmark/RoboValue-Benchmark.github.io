Model identification marks

Original project and institution marks are used to identify the evaluated
models. Ownership remains with the respective projects and institutions.
The images are copied from the official sources below, without redrawing.
Shared model variants use the same family logo.

Where a dedicated project mark could not be identified, the logo is from
the first institution listed on the official project page or paper.
Source details are also recorded in src/data/model-logos.json.
